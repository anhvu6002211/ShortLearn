# Terms of Use — ShortLearn Extension

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

ShortLearn Extension is a learning aid. It displays available captions, translations, vocabulary tools, and reviews through the existing ShortLearn account. Translations and captions may be incomplete or inaccurate and must not be treated as professional advice.

Users must follow YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Chrome Web Store, applicable copyright, and local laws. ShortLearn does not bypass login, DRM, CAPTCHA, geographic restrictions, or platform access controls.

AI Live Captions is experimental. Tab capture starts only after an explicit user action. The current MVP does not upload or transcribe captured audio. Any future remote speech processing requires separate, explicit consent and updated disclosure.

Users remain responsible for their ShortLearn credentials and API endpoint choice. HTTPS is required except local development. ShortLearn may stop supporting a site when its public web interface changes.

The extension is provided as-is during MVP testing. To stop local processing, stop capture, sign out, disable, or uninstall the extension. Account-data deletion follows the ShortLearn service policy and backend account controls.
